If you are new to home efficiency, start with the Energy Saving category. It focuses on broad, practical improvements before moving into specific devices, lighting upgrades, or heating and cooling changes.

For projects involving electrical work, HVAC systems, insulation, structural changes, or safety-sensitive tasks, consult a qualified professional before making changes.
